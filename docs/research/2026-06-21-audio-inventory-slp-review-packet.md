## What Reader's Way is asking

Please review the proposed recording inventory before capture if your availability permits.

child.

## Decisions needing professional review

Please focus on:

1. Whether each stable sound target is instructionally appropriate as an isolated model.
2. Whether `/θ/` and `/ð/` guidance is clear and distinct.
3. Whether stop and affricate guidance avoids added schwa.
4. Whether `/ŋ/` guidance avoids an appended `/g/`.
5. Whether the schwa target can be responsibly modeled in isolation or should be derived
   from a carrier word.
6. Whether UFLI's `/ju/` target should be recorded as one instructional sequence.
7. Whether the rhotic targets `/ɑr/`, `/ɝ/`, and `/ɔr/` are appropriate for the selected
   rhotic reference.
8. Whether the `/ʍ/` (“wh”) target should remain distinct even though wine–whine merger is
   common.
9. Whether preserving separate `/ɑ/` and `/ɔ/` models is appropriate given cot–caught
   merger and the likely recorder's dialect.
10. Whether any example word creates avoidable coarticulation, dialect, or ambiguity.
11. Whether clip/sustain/glide/sequence guidance should change.
12. Whether any target should be added, removed, combined, or renamed.

## Grapheme mappings needing review

Please verify:

- `th` maps to both `/θ/` and `/ð/`;
- `qu` is represented as `/k/` + `/w/`, not one sound;
- `ck`, `ll`, `ss`, `ff`, and `zz` reuse existing sound targets;
- `ng` maps to `/ŋ/` for the primary instructional correspondence;
- `wh` mapping follows the approved dialect/reference decision; and
- alternate values for `ch`, `ng`, `ss`, and `ph` are documented without overloading the
  initial beta scope.
